Roles & Access Model

The foundation every dashboard reads from. Today there are three roles; we add one.

Today

Current roles

  • ADMIN — full access, manages BDOs & users.
  • CEO — executive read access.
  • BDO — own dashboard, own pipeline & tasks.

A login (User) optionally links to a BDO profile via bdoId; the BDO profile carries the company.

Proposed

Add: Senior BDO

  • SENIOR_BDO — a BDO who also leads a team within a company.
  • Sees their own numbers plus every BDO they supervise.
  • Needs a link from team members to their lead (e.g. managerId on the BDO profile).

A

Staff Accounts

Move from "invite a BDO" to "invite any staff member" with the correct role and company.

Now

Where we are

  • Admin can create User logins and BDO profiles.
  • Roles exist (ADMIN/CEO/BDO) but the invite flow is BDO-centric.
Target

Where we're going

  • One "Add staff" flow: name, email, role, company.
  • Auto-generated temp password + forced change on first login (already supported by mustChangePassword).
  • Senior BDO can be assigned team members.

Steps

  • Add SENIOR_BDO to the User.role enum and to the invite form's role selector.
  • Add managerId (nullable) to the BDO profile so members can point to their Senior BDO.
  • Extend the Manage Users screen: filter by role & company, show status, resend invite / reset password.
  • On invite, email or display the temporary credentials for hand-off.

Data model

  • User: role enum → ['ADMIN','CEO','SENIOR_BDO','BDO'].
  • BDO: add managerId: string | null.

Done when

  • Admin can create an account for any role and any of the six companies.
  • A Senior BDO can be linked to the members they supervise.
  • New staff are forced to set a password on first login.
B

KPI Framework — Companies & BDOs

KPIs "for all" (each company) and "for each BDO" (individual targets vs actuals).

Now

What exists

  • KpiScorecard — monthly company KPIs, but the company enum is locked to BAPX / TRADELINE only.
  • MonthlyKpi — already has bdoId, metric, target, actual: the per-BDO foundation is present but not surfaced.
Target

What we build

  • Company KPIs for all six companies.
  • Per-BDO KPI targets vs actuals, visible on each BDO dashboard.
  • Rollups: a BDO's actuals roll up to their company; companies roll up to the group.

Steps

  • Widen KpiScorecard.company enum to all six company keys (mirrors the fix already done for BDOs & tasks).
  • Update the KPI Scorecard page filters/form to list all companies (currently hardcoded to BAPX/Tradeline).
  • Build a per-BDO KPI editor on top of MonthlyKpi (metric, month, target, actual, notes).
  • Surface per-BDO KPI tiles (target vs actual, % attainment, on-track/at-risk/off-track) on the BDO dashboard.
  • Define the standard metric set once (e.g. deals won, revenue, calls/meetings, conversion) so every BDO is measured the same way.
Decision needed: the standard KPI metric list. Give me the metrics you want every BDO measured on and I'll seed them so the scorecards are consistent instead of free-text.

Done when

  • Company KPI scorecards can be entered for all six companies.
  • Each BDO has monthly targets vs actuals visible on their dashboard.
  • Attainment status (on-track / at-risk / off-track) is computed, not typed.

Senior BDO Dashboard

A team-lead view sitting between the individual BDO view and the executive view.

What it shows — scoped to the members linked via managerId:

  • Team pipeline: combined deals, stages, health, potential vs received revenue.
  • Team KPI board: each member's target vs actual, ranked, with team totals.
  • Scheme-of-work rollup: open / overdue / completed tasks across the team.
  • Drill-down: click a member to open their individual dashboard.

Steps

  • New route /senior/[id] (or reuse the BDO dashboard with a "team" tab when role is SENIOR_BDO).
  • API: list deals/tasks/KPIs where the BDO's managerId = the senior BDO (plus the senior's own).
  • Reuse existing pipeline charts & stat tiles; add a per-member leaderboard table (uses the paginated DataTable).
  • Sidebar: show a "My Team" link when the logged-in user is a Senior BDO.

Done when

  • A Senior BDO logs in and sees their team's combined pipeline, revenue and KPIs.
  • They can drill into any team member's own dashboard.
  • They cannot see other teams or other companies.
D

Executive Dashboard

The CEO/board screen: the whole group on one page, at a glance.

What it shows

  • Group headline tiles: total potential / received / outstanding revenue across all six companies.
  • Revenue & deal trend charts over time.
  • KPI health summary — how many company/BDO KPIs are on-track vs at-risk vs off-track.
  • Top deals and top risks (biggest at-risk / stale deals) pulled from every pipeline.
  • Per-company snapshot strip linking through to each company's detail.

Steps

  • New route /executive, restricted to CEO/Admin.
  • API aggregation endpoint that sums across BapxDeal + TradelineDeal + CompanyDeal and KPI collections.
  • Build tiles + Recharts trend/health charts; reuse revenue formatting already used on pipelines.
  • Add to the CEO sidebar as the landing view.
Note: the current home page (/) is already a "CEO Dashboard." We should decide whether the Executive dashboard replaces it or sits alongside it as a deeper, group-wide view. My recommendation: make Executive the richer group view and keep the current page as the simpler landing.

Done when

  • The CEO sees group-wide revenue, trends and KPI health without opening each company.
  • Top deals and top risks surface automatically.

F

Payment Structure Module

Bring the payment method/schedule from the Excel sheet into the system.

The deal collections already track revenue, received amounts and outstanding balances. This workstream adds the how — the payment method, instalments/milestones and schedule described in your spreadsheet — so a deal's money can be tracked against a defined plan rather than a single number.

Steps (once the sheet is shared)

  • Map the spreadsheet columns → a PaymentPlan / PaymentSchedule model (milestone, due date, amount, % , status).
  • Attach payment plans to deals; compute received vs outstanding from the schedule.
  • Optional one-time importer to load the existing Excel rows so history isn't retyped.
  • Surface a payment tab on the deal / pipeline view and roll paid/overdue amounts into the dashboards above.
Blocked on input. I've left this deliberately light because the model must match your actual sheet. Share it and I'll turn its columns into the exact schema and screens — that avoids a guess-and-redo cycle.

Done when

  • A deal can carry a structured payment plan matching the Excel sheet.
  • Received vs outstanding is computed from the schedule, not entered by hand.
  • Overdue payments surface on the relevant dashboards.
